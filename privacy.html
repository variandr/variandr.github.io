Privacy Policy: YT Personal Assistant

Last updated: 27 October 2026

What the app accesses

With the account holder's consent through Google sign-in, the app uses YouTube API Services to upload videos, set titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and publish times, and to read channel and video statistics and analytics for the signed-in YouTube channel.

How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to manage the owner's own channels. Access tokens and analytics reports are stored locally on the owner's computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at Google Account permissions. Stored data is deleted on request.

Google and YouTube

This app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
